Scope: single-line scalars (integers, floats, booleans, date-times, basic and literal strings). Arrays, inline tables, tables and multi-line strings are not parsed. Follows TOML 1.0.0: 0755, .5, 5., +0x10, True and yes are all errors. Big integers beyond 64 bits are rejected as the spec requires. Runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
